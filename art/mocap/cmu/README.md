Motion capture from the CMU Graphics Lab Motion Capture Database (http://mocap.cs.cmu.edu),
BVH conversion from github.com/una-dinosauria/cmu-mocap. Free to use, including in
commercial products; the raw data may not be resold as a motion database.
"The data used in this project was obtained from mocap.cs.cmu.edu.
The database was created with funding from NSF EIA-0196217."
Retargeted to our rig by tools/cmu_retarget.py (via tools/reshape_character.py).
